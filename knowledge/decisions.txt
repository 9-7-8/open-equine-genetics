DECISIONS ALREADY MADE
======================
All made by the owner (Isabel) on 2026-10-12 unless a line says otherwise. The full record
is GitHub issue #250 in 9-7-8/Procedural-Horse-Coats. Do not reopen one without asking her.

PURPOSE
- A separate repo she maintains, usable in other projects, language-agnostic.
- Aim: the most accurate list of genes and breeds for real horses, kept up to date with
  UC Davis findings as far as she can.

WHAT IT HOLDS
- Real science, uncollapsed where it is known.
- PLUS the mod's abstractions, each clearly labelled as an abstraction. An abstraction is a
  single made-up locus standing in for something real that is polygenic or not yet
  understood (height, flaxen, sooty). They are named "abstracted ...", never "magical ...".
- A "mod map" that says how the mod's model relates to the data here.
- Nothing invented for the game with no real counterpart. No magical genes.

WHICH GENES
- Every gene with a published locus.
- As abstractions: flaxen, shade, sooty, markings, pangare, rabicano, manchado, CVM,
  megaesophagus, PATN2, the eight eye loci, fertility, natural zebra (as a HORSE locus).
- As abstractions from the mod's body stats: abstracted height, speed, jump, hardiness,
  draft power. The mod's "vampiric" allele on those is not included.
- Left out on purpose: diet; climate tolerance; temperament; yields; everything magical.

SPECIES
- Equids only: horse now; donkey and zebra may follow. No sheep.

BREEDS (not exported yet)
- Natural breeds only, with nothing Minecraft in them (no biomes, spawn rules, prices).
- Gene pools: each frequency carries a citation where one exists and an "estimated" flag
  where it is a guess. Everything starts as estimated.
- Body: real measurements (height, weight, type) with citations, AND the mod's 1-10 scores
  for speed, jump, health and pull, marked as abstracted estimates.

EVIDENCE
- Every entry says how far to trust it. See knowledge/format.txt.
- Citations come first from the mod's wiki Science tabs, harvested once. Those tabs stay
  hand-written in the mod; generating them from this data was declined for now and may be
  asked again.

THE AUDIT
- Making the data accurate is its own job (issue #256), locus by locus, against UC Davis
  VGL, OMIA and the papers.
- THE SCIENCE WINS (2026-10-12). "Always go with the science. If science contradicts an
  early definition of a gene, update it to be as accurate to the science as we possibly
  can." When the owner supplies research, it is applied to the gene file without asking
  her locus by locus. She is asked only what science cannot settle: the format, what
  becomes of an allele or locus the science does not support (ids are never removed),
  the mod, and commits.
- An outcome nobody has described is marked as undocumented, never guessed.
- Every allele carries a quality tag: verified, unverified, abstracted or invalid. An
  allele the science does not support is kept and marked invalid, so that nobody adds
  it back thinking it was overlooked. (agouti's At and A+, proposed but never validated,
  are tagged this way. mitf SW9 was the first, until a laboratory was found that lists
  an SW9; see the ruling on it below.)
- Citations are not all to be opened and checked for now; each is marked verified or
  not. None is verified yet.
- A condition goes on a genotype only where a source confirms it for that pair. (mitf:
  no deafness on any pair.)
- A locus named for a gene it has nothing to do with is renamed (ryr2 became
  abstracted_jump_strength).
- The mod will come to this repo to bridge itself once the work here is done. Nothing
  here waits on the mod, and nobody here needs to tell it of each change.
- Commit now, often, and in small batches. Push when the session ends (above).
- A frequency nobody has published is left out, never invented: a locus may have no
  frequency table.
- Where the research lists two variants and the data has one allele, the two are added as
  new alleles and the old id is kept and marked invalid (2026-10-12: kit W17 became W17a
  and W17b; shox sa became Del1 and Del2).
  When later research shows the split alleles are one allele after all, the old id is
  restored and the split ids are marked invalid in its place (owner, 2026-10-15: kit W17
  restored, W17a and W17b invalid, after round three found them always together).
- An allele the research does not mention, or calls disputed, is kept as "unconfirmed" or
  "disputed" with quality "unverified" and no outcome; it is not marked invalid
  (2026-10-12: kit W37-W39, since confirmed by a second answer; matp sunshine). pax3
  SW10 was treated the same way by the session, without asking.
- A severity claims only what the research states, and follows the research when it
  says more (all 2026-10-12). Skeletal atavism (shox) was "impairing" while the research
  stated no death; it is "lethal_at_birth" since her round-two answer said affected foals
  are usually nonviable or put down shortly after birth. ACAN D1/D1 is
  "lethal_in_early_life", not the mod's "lethal_at_birth": the same answer said its
  survival time varies.
- An allele marked invalid because nothing was found for it becomes "unconfirmed" once a
  source is found that lists it, even without a published report (2026-10-12: mitf SW9,
  after round two found European laboratories listing an SW9 and an SW10). acan D5, for
  which round two found no report at all, was made "unconfirmed" by the session on the
  earlier ruling, without asking.
