DECISIONS ALREADY MADE
======================
All made by the owner (Isabel) on 2026-10-12 unless a line says otherwise. Do not reopen
one without asking her.

PURPOSE
- A separate repo she maintains, usable in other projects, language-agnostic. It stands
  on its own (owner, 2026-10-15): where the data first came from is not part of it.
- Aim: the most accurate list of genes and breeds for real horses, kept up to date with
  UC Davis findings as far as she can.

WHAT IT HOLDS
- Real science, uncollapsed where it is known.
- PLUS abstractions, each clearly labelled as an abstraction. An abstraction is a single
  made-up locus standing in for something real that is polygenic or not yet understood
  (height, flaxen, sooty). They are named "abstracted ...", never "magical ...".
- Nothing invented with no real counterpart. No magical genes.

WHICH GENES
- Every gene with a published locus.
- As abstractions: flaxen, shade, sooty, markings, pangare, rabicano, manchado, CVM,
  megaesophagus, PATN2, the eight eye loci, fertility, natural zebra (as a HORSE locus).
- As abstractions of body traits: abstracted height, speed, jump, hardiness, draft power.
- Left out on purpose: diet; climate tolerance; temperament; yields; everything magical.

SPECIES
- Equids only: horse now; donkey and zebra may follow. No sheep.

BREEDS
- Natural breeds only (no biomes, spawn rules or prices).
- Gene pools: each frequency carries a citation where one exists and an "estimated" flag
  where it is a guess. Everything starts as estimated.
- Body: real measurements (height, weight, type) with citations, AND 1-10 scores for
  speed, jump, health and pull, marked as abstracted estimates.

EVIDENCE
- Every entry says how far to trust it. See knowledge/format.txt.

THE AUDIT
- Making the data accurate is its own job, locus by locus, against UC Davis VGL, OMIA and
  the papers.
- THE SCIENCE WINS (2026-10-12). "Always go with the science. If science contradicts an
  early definition of a gene, update it to be as accurate to the science as we possibly
  can." When the owner supplies research, it is applied to the gene file without asking
  her locus by locus. She is asked only what science cannot settle: the format, what
  becomes of an allele or locus the science does not support (ids are never removed),
  and commits.
- An outcome nobody has described is marked as undocumented, never guessed.
- Every allele carries a quality tag: verified, unverified, abstracted or invalid. An
  allele the science does not support is kept and marked invalid, so that nobody adds
  it back thinking it was overlooked. (shox sa and kit W17a and W17b are tagged this way.)
- An allele that is proposed but not validated - a real look, or a source that hedges -
  is "unverified", named "proposed", "unconfirmed" or "disputed", with an undocumented
  outcome. Not "invalid", which says no source supports it (owner, 2026-10-15: agouti
  At and A+, which were "invalid" from 2026-10-12 until then).
- Citations are not all to be opened and checked for now; each is marked verified or
  not. None is verified yet.
- A condition goes on a genotype only where a source confirms it for that pair. (mitf:
  no deafness on any pair.)
- A locus named for a gene it has nothing to do with is renamed (ryr2 became
  abstracted_jump_strength).
- Commit now, often, and in small batches, grouping like things (2026-10-15). Push when
  the session ends (above).
- A frequency nobody has published is left out, never invented: a locus may have no
  frequency table.
- An allele known to be common but with no exact figure per breed is left out of every
  table, its copies counted as the wild type, rather than given a share that is known
  to be wrong (owner, 2026-10-15: matp sun, the major allele in every breed group
  sequenced, whose effect is unresolved).
- Where the research lists two variants and the data has one allele, the two are added as
  new alleles and the old id is kept and marked invalid (2026-10-12: shox sa became Del1
  and Del2; kit W17 became W17a and W17b).
  When later research shows the split alleles are one allele after all, the old id is
  restored and the split ids are marked invalid in its place (owner, 2026-10-15: kit W17
  restored, W17a and W17b invalid, after round three found them always together).
- An allele the research does not mention, or calls disputed, is kept as "unconfirmed" or
  "disputed" with quality "unverified" and no outcome; it is not marked invalid
  (2026-10-12: kit W37-W39, since confirmed; matp sunshine). An unconfirmed allele that
  research later publishes becomes an ordinary allele (2026-10-15: mitf SW9 and pax3
  SW10).
- A severity claims only what the research states, and follows the research when it
  says more. Skeletal atavism (shox) was "impairing" while the research stated no death;
  it is "lethal_at_birth" since her round-two answer said affected foals are usually
  nonviable or put down shortly after birth. ACAN D1/D1 was "lethal_in_early_life" on
  round two's "survival time varies"; since round three (2026-10-15) it is nonviable,
  dying before birth, with D1/D2 and D1/D3.
- An allele marked invalid because nothing was found for it becomes "unconfirmed" once a
  source is found that lists it, even without a published report (2026-10-12: mitf SW9).
  acan D5, for which round two found no report at all, was made "unconfirmed" by the
  session on the earlier ruling, without asking.
