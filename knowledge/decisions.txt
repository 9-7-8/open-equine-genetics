DECISIONS ALREADY MADE
======================
All made by the owner (Isabel) on 2026-10-12 unless a line says otherwise. The full record
is GitHub issue #250 in 9-7-8/Procedural-Horse-Coats. Do not reopen one without asking her.

PURPOSE
- A separate repo she maintains, usable in other projects, language-agnostic.
- Aim: the most accurate list of genes and breeds for real horses, kept up to date with
  UC Davis findings as far as she can.

WHAT IT HOLDS
- Real science, uncollapsed where it is known.
- PLUS the mod's abstractions, each clearly labelled as an abstraction. An abstraction is a
  single made-up locus standing in for something real that is polygenic or not yet
  understood (height, flaxen, sooty). They are named "abstracted ...", never "magical ...".
- A "mod map" that says how the mod's model relates to the data here.
- Nothing invented for the game with no real counterpart. No magical genes.

WHICH GENES
- Every gene with a published locus.
- As abstractions: flaxen, shade, sooty, markings, pangare, rabicano, manchado, CVM,
  megaesophagus, PATN2, the eight eye loci, fertility, natural zebra (as a HORSE locus).
- As abstractions from the mod's body stats: abstracted height, speed, jump, hardiness,
  draft power. The mod's "vampiric" allele on those is not included.
- Left out on purpose: diet; climate tolerance; temperament; yields; everything magical.

SPECIES
- Equids only: horse now; donkey and zebra may follow. No sheep.

BREEDS (not exported yet)
- Natural breeds only, with nothing Minecraft in them (no biomes, spawn rules, prices).
- Gene pools: each frequency carries a citation where one exists and an "estimated" flag
  where it is a guess. Everything starts as estimated.
- Body: real measurements (height, weight, type) with citations, AND the mod's 1-10 scores
  for speed, jump, health and pull, marked as abstracted estimates.

EVIDENCE
- Every entry says how far to trust it. See knowledge/format.txt.
- Citations come first from the mod's wiki Science tabs, harvested once. Those tabs stay
  hand-written in the mod; generating them from this data was declined for now and may be
  asked again.

LICENCE
- GPL 3.
- Contributions: a clause in CONTRIBUTING and a pull-request checkbox - "I license this
  contribution under GPL 3 and grant the maintainer the right to relicense it." This is
  what lets outside additions flow back into the MIT-licensed mod.
- Inside the mod's jar the data stays GPL 3.
- Repo name: open-equine-genetics. Public, under her GitHub account. Created and first
  pushed 2026-10-12: github.com/9-7-8/open-equine-genetics.

THE REPOSITORY (2026-10-12)
- Everything in the folder is committed EXCEPT session-logs/, procedures/ and (from
  later the same day) TODO.txt. Those stay on the maintainer's machine. mod-map.json, knowledge/science/ and
  knowledge/how-this-was-exported/ are published.
- No local file path goes in anything committed. Paths already in the commit history
  stay; history is not rewritten for them.
- Pushes are asked for each time; there is no standing permission to push.

THE MOD
- The mod will READ this repo as its source of truth (not merely export to it), through a
  git submodule pinned to a commit.
- Saved gene keys and allele tokens in the mod never change; mod-map.json translates.
- Until the mod reads it (issue #253 onward), this repo is a MIRROR: a change in the mod
  has to be re-exported here, and a change here does not reach the mod.

THE AUDIT
- Making the data accurate is its own job (issue #256): locus by locus, a session
  researches UC Davis VGL, OMIA and the papers, and the owner approves each change.

MADE BY THE EXPORTING SESSION, NOT ASKED (change freely, tell the owner)
- The file layout and every field name in knowledge/format.txt.
- genes/ is one flat folder with a "species" field in each file.
- "sex" is not exported as a locus.
- The gene symbols filled into "symbol" (see known-issues.txt).
- This folder living beside the mod's folder, not inside it.
