KNOWN ISSUES IN THE DATA
========================
Rewritten 2026-10-12, when the owner's research began to be applied. Remove a line
here when it is fixed, and say so in the session log.

WHERE THE DATA STANDS
- genes/ began as an export of what a game models (knowledge/how-this-was-exported/, mod
  commit 93af89d9 plus one docs commit). On 2026-10-12 the owner supplied research
  documents and audit answers, and the loci below were changed to follow them. The three
  lists in this file are each kept on one long line, sorted, so they are easy to edit.
RESEARCHED: abstracted_jump_strength, acan, agouti, b4galt7, brindle, champagne, ckm, dmrt3, ednrb, grey, hmga2, lama3, lamc2, lcorl, leopard, mitf, mstn, patn1, pdk4, roan, ryr1, scn4a, silver, slc5a3.
- NO SESSION OPENED A SOURCE. Every fact changed that day rests on the owner's documents,
  and every citation added is an address those documents gave. Some addresses were built
  from a DOI or a PubMed number the documents gave. None has been opened.
- The owner's rule (knowledge/decisions.txt): the science wins. Where it contradicts
  what a locus said before, the locus is changed.
- Every other locus is still the mod's export, unaudited.
- Since format 2 every allele says how far to trust it ("quality") and every citation
  says whether a maintainer has opened it ("verified"). As of 2026-10-12: no citation
  is verified, no allele is "verified", three alleles are "invalid" (mitf SW9, agouti At
  and A+), and every
  allele of an abstracted locus is "abstracted". The owner said not to spend effort
  opening every citation for now.
- tools/validate.mjs passes on all 68 files. It checks structure, not science.

THE MOD NO LONGER MATCHES
While this repo is a mirror, a re-export from the mod would undo all of this. The owner
has said the mod's side will come to this repo to bridge itself once the work here is
done (2026-10-12); nothing is owed to it before then. The mod still has:
- brindle as a two-copy trait with white stripes, and a 10 percent founder rate;
- melanoma risk on grey's G2, and no slow/fast split by allele;
- mitf: SW3 giving more white than SW1, certain deafness on two copies, living
  SW5/SW5, outcomes for pairs of two different variants, and no SW6, SW7, SW8;
- scn4a: two copies killing the foal;
- ryr1: a severe living two-copy form; a real jumping gene it calls ryr2;
- pdk4 as a plain speed gene; hmga2 and lcorl changing speed and jump;
- acan carriers of normal height; b4galt7 dwarfs that are slow and jump badly;
- agouti with a as the wild type, and bay split into four shades inside the agouti
  table.- champagne feeding removed black back as red, and an eye colour inherited with the allele (olive green among them);- ckm as a speed gene;- dmrt3 one-copy horses that cannot pace, and "the smoothest gaits" as a fixed property of two copies;
mod-map.json records the structural part: "not_in_mod" (alleles) and
"mod_outcome_unsupported" (pairs marked undocumented here on purpose).

STILL WRONG OR UNPROVEN IN THE RESEARCHED LOCI
- mitf SW9 is marked "invalid" (owner, 2026-10-12): no published allele, laboratory or
  paper was found for it, and the name may be a mix-up with W9 of KIT. The id is kept so
  nobody adds it back. Its eight pairs have the outcome "invalid-allele".
- mitf: 18 pairs of two different variants are "splash-undocumented" and 5 homozygotes
  (SW3, SW5, SW6, SW7, SW8) are "splash-homozygote-undetected". Nothing is published
  for them. No genotype carries a deafness condition any more (owner, 2026-10-12: only
  pairs a source confirms; none is confirmed). SW5's raised risk is in the summary.
- abstracted_jump_strength was "ryr2" until 2026-10-12 (owner: rename it). The mod's
  key in mod-map.json is still horsegenetics.ryr2. The data now has two abstractions
  for jumping, this and abstracted_jump; whether both are wanted is open.
- roan: the symbol stays KIT. The science says KIT region, causal variant unknown;
  RN1, RN2 and RN3 are haplotype markers, named in the summary only.
- acan: D5 has only a genomic coordinate, and the research flags it unverified. The
  UC Davis page for the ACAN test was not found (the Friesian dwarfism page is a
  different test). That D1/D1 is lethal at birth and the rest survive is the mod's.
- ryr1: two papers are cited as the first description (2004 and 2009); the research
  says the 2004 one is the primary and the 2009 reference may have been a confusion.
- grey: G2/G3 is given the same outcome as G3/N; the research says its pace and risk
  are not quantified. The Andalusian table's genotype shares are worked out from
  allele frequencies, assuming random mating. So is ryr1's Quarter Horse table.
- silver: chromosome 6 comes from a commercial laboratory's page. That a chestnut
  carrier has the same eye risk is reasoned from the genotype, not from a study that
  compared them. That silver is hidden on a chestnut is in words only.
- leopard still depends on patn2, an abstraction; no real second modifier was found.
- pdk4: G is flagged wild_type because the format needs one; no source says which
  allele is ancestral.
- slc5a3, ryr1, grey: the "general population" tables are still the mod's.
- Frequencies: brindle has none, on purpose. Published tables exist only for slc5a3
  (Fell, Dales), ryr1 (Quarter Horse), grey (Andalusian) and b4galt7 (Friesian).
- agouti: At (seal brown) and A+ (wild bay) are in the data marked "invalid" (owner,
  2026-10-12). The research calls them plausible but unproven rather than wrong, and
  some commercial laboratories report At; "invalid" is the nearest tag the format has.
- Bay shade is now stated nowhere as a table: agouti gives bay or black, and the
  abstracted shade locus describes its effect in its summary only. The research
  supports a real shade modifier upstream of ASIP near RALY (chromosome 22), cause
  unidentified, with E/E bays tending darker than E/e.
- b4galt7: no chromosome was given. Its research names its sources by number only, so
  no citation was added; the two it already had (UC Davis, Leegwater et al.) are from
  the first export. The Friesian table omits d/d horses, which the 12 percent carrier
  estimate does not count.
- ednrb and hmga2 had one correction each and are otherwise unresearched.
- champagne: the names gold, amber and classic champagne depend on extension and agouti; the file says so in words and has no interactions table. "Sable" (on seal brown) and "ivory" (with cream) are registry names, not alleles, and are not in the data.
- ckm: a candidate that failed to replicate (Gu et al. 2010; no association in Quarter Horses). The file states no effect. The variant is written g.15884567G>A in one source and g.22999655C>A in another, not reconciled. C is flagged wild_type only because the format needs one. Whether a locus with no established effect belongs in the data as published is the owner's call.
- dmrt3: the Icelandic table's genotype shares are worked out from one allele frequency, assuming random mating. A 2026 study reports further variants in exon 2 of DMRT3, of unknown effect; none is in the data. Other gait loci (trot versus pace in Standardbreds) are known to exist and are not modelled.

THE OTHER LOCI
- "symbol" was filled from the exporting session's own knowledge. The research has since
  confirmed tiger_eye (SLC24A5), mushroom (MFSD12), patn1 (RFWD3), brindle (MBTPS2) and
  tobiano as a KIT-region locus. The rest are unchecked.
- Published loci with no research from the owner yet:
NOT YET RESEARCHED: dun, extension, gbe1, gys1, kit, matp, met, mushroom, myo5a, pax3, plod1, ppib, prkdc, rapgef5, shox, st14, tiger_eye, tobiano, toe1.
- Game wording: the plain cases were reworded on 2026-10-12 by word search. Subtler
  cases remain: what "wild horses" or founders carry (eye_colour_left,
  eye_colour_right, manchado, pax3, plod1, rapgef5) and "no wild horse is born with
  two" in the five abstracted body loci.
- The five abstracted body loci describe "the percentage written on that copy". In the
  mod each allele copy carries its own inherited strength; that idea is not in this
  format. Owner's decision.
- Collapsed loci: kit (dominant white W alleles, sabino and more as one locus) and matp
  are in the mod's shape. Real alleles are missing, e.g. "ea" at extension.
- Dosage loci (shade, flaxen, sooty, pangare, markings) are the mod's model.
- Lethal pairs: kit, met and pax3 list theirs as nonviable; check each against a source.
  The research says no horse with two copies of SW4 (pax3) has been detected either.
- fertility: no single-locus basis for twinning or subfertility was found. It is an
  abstraction and is marked as one.
- Every frequency table is the mod's wild-horse founder rate, marked "estimated".

CITATIONS
- Every published locus has at least one citation; no abstracted locus has any.
- For the 29 loci cited by the first export, the citations are every external link in
  that gene's wiki page, attached to the whole locus, with "source" guessed from the
  host name. For the 14 cited on 2026-10-12 they are the owner's addresses, unopened.
- A citation hangs on the locus, not on an allele, a genotype or a table.
- tools/validate.mjs does not check that a published locus has a citation.

INTERACTIONS
- Only agouti, leopard and flaxen carry an interactions table. agouti's is with
  extension alone since 2026-10-12.
- Real epistasis NOT stated in the data: grey and dominant white hiding other colours,
  cream on red versus black, silver showing only on black pigment.

STRUCTURE
- "chromosome" is only on loci whose research gave one. To list them:
  grep -l '"chromosome"' genes/*.json
- "sex" is not a locus here. brindle is x_linked and uses a placeholder allele "Y".
- The left-eye loci in the mod usually copy the right eye; that is not expressed here.
- tools/validate.mjs does not check that allele order is unchanged since the last
  version (there is no tagged version to compare with yet).
- 36 allele names keep the allele id, because the name alone would not tell two alleles
  of the locus apart ("Dominant white W22"). Others carry a laboratory's label in
  brackets ("Wild-type (G1)").
