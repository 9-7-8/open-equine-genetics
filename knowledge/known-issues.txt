KNOWN ISSUES IN THE DATA
========================
Rewritten 2026-10-12, when the owner's research began to be applied. Remove a line
here when it is fixed, and say so in the session log.

WHERE THE DATA STANDS
- genes/ began as an export of what a game models (knowledge/how-this-was-exported/, mod
  commit 93af89d9 plus one docs commit). On 2026-10-12 the owner supplied research
  documents and audit answers, and the loci below were changed to follow them. The three
  lists in this file are each kept on one long line, sorted, so they are easy to edit.
RESEARCHED: abstracted_jump_strength, acan, agouti, b4galt7, brindle, champagne, ckm, dmrt3, dun, ednrb, extension, gbe1, grey, gys1, hmga2, kit, lama3, lamc2, lcorl, leopard, matp, met, mitf, mstn, mushroom, myo5a, patn1, pax3, pdk4, plod1, ppib, prkdc, rapgef5, roan, ryr1, scn4a, shox, silver, slc5a3, st14, tiger_eye, tobiano.
- NO SESSION OPENED A SOURCE. Every fact changed that day rests on the owner's documents,
  and every citation added is an address those documents gave. Some addresses were built
  from a DOI or a PubMed number the documents gave. None has been opened.
- The owner's rule (knowledge/decisions.txt): the science wins. Where it contradicts
  what a locus said before, the locus is changed.
- Every other locus is still the mod's export, unaudited.
- Since format 2 every allele says how far to trust it ("quality") and every citation
  says whether a maintainer has opened it ("verified"). As of 2026-10-12: no citation
  is verified, no allele is "verified", five alleles are "invalid" (mitf SW9, agouti At
  and A+, kit W17, shox sa), and every
  allele of an abstracted locus is "abstracted". The owner said not to spend effort
  opening every citation for now.
- tools/validate.mjs passes on all 68 files. It checks structure, not science.

THE MOD NO LONGER MATCHES
While this repo is a mirror, a re-export from the mod would undo all of this. The owner
has said the mod's side will come to this repo to bridge itself once the work here is
done (2026-10-12); nothing is owed to it before then. The mod still has:
- brindle as a two-copy trait with white stripes, and a 10 percent founder rate;
- melanoma risk on grey's G2, and no slow/fast split by allele;
- mitf: SW3 giving more white than SW1, certain deafness on two copies, living
  SW5/SW5, outcomes for pairs of two different variants, and no SW6, SW7, SW8;
- scn4a: two copies killing the foal;
- ryr1: a severe living two-copy form; a real jumping gene it calls ryr2;
- pdk4 as a plain speed gene; hmga2 and lcorl changing speed and jump;
- acan carriers of normal height; b4galt7 dwarfs that are slow and jump badly;
- agouti with a as the wild type, and bay split into four shades inside the agouti
  table.
- champagne feeding removed black back as red, and an eye colour inherited with the allele (olive green among them);
- ckm as a speed gene;
- dmrt3 one-copy horses that cannot pace, and "the smoothest gaits" as a fixed property of two copies;
- dun: d2 as the wild type, red pigment left undiluted (a red dun nearly chestnut), a certain dorsal stripe on every d1 horse, and "two dominance orders".
- gbe1 at one carrier rate for every horse, where the allele is confined to Quarter Horse lines;
- kit: 30 homozygotes as certainly nonviable and W4/W4 as a living white horse; an outcome for every pair of two different variants; W1, W3, W7, W13, W16, W23, W25, W27, W30 as mostly white, not all white; a speckled pattern for W8, W18, W21; W4 as Camarillo white.
- matp: sunshine as a rare recessive dilution that looks like champagne; cream beside sunshine or snowdrop, and pearl beside snowdrop, as a double dilute; pearl beside sunshine as pearl; two pearl copies leaving red barely touched; one phenotype for Cr/Cr and Cr/prl.
- gys1 with every copy showing, and one rate for every breed, Thoroughbreds and Arabians included;
- mushroom trading red pigment partly for black, and one carrier rate for every breed;
- met at one carrier rate for every breed, where it is reported in Friesians only.
- myo5a with no coat effect on the affected foal, and one carrier rate for every breed;
- plod1 foals always born alive, and one carrier rate for every breed;
- ppib at one carrier rate for every breed, where it belongs to Quarter Horse lines.
- extension without ea.
- shox with one allele, sa, and skeletal atavism as lethal at birth;
- pax3: SW10 as a splash allele; SW4/SW4 and SW10/SW10 as certainly nonviable; SW2/SW4 as a living bold splash; certain deafness on two copies.
- tiger_eye at one rate for every breed, and two TE2 copies as a settled paler yellow.
- st14 as lethal at birth, at one carrier rate for every breed.
- prkdc as lethal at birth, at one carrier rate for every breed.
- rapgef5 as lethal at birth, at one carrier rate for every breed.
mod-map.json records the structural part: "not_in_mod" (alleles) and
"mod_outcome_unsupported" (pairs marked undocumented here on purpose).

STILL WRONG OR UNPROVEN IN THE RESEARCHED LOCI
- mitf SW9 is marked "invalid" (owner, 2026-10-12): no published allele, laboratory or
  paper was found for it, and the name may be a mix-up with W9 of KIT. The id is kept so
  nobody adds it back. Its eight pairs have the outcome "invalid-allele".
- mitf: 18 pairs of two different variants are "splash-undocumented" and 5 homozygotes
  (SW3, SW5, SW6, SW7, SW8) are "splash-homozygote-undetected". Nothing is published
  for them. No genotype carries a deafness condition any more (owner, 2026-10-12: only
  pairs a source confirms; none is confirmed). SW5's raised risk is in the summary.
- abstracted_jump_strength was "ryr2" until 2026-10-12 (owner: rename it). The mod's
  key in mod-map.json is still horsegenetics.ryr2. The data now has two abstractions
  for jumping, this and abstracted_jump; whether both are wanted is open.
- roan: the symbol stays KIT. The science says KIT region, causal variant unknown;
  RN1, RN2 and RN3 are haplotype markers, named in the summary only.
- acan: D5 has only a genomic coordinate, and the research flags it unverified. The
  UC Davis page for the ACAN test was not found (the Friesian dwarfism page is a
  different test). That D1/D1 is lethal at birth and the rest survive is the mod's.
- ryr1: two papers are cited as the first description (2004 and 2009); the research
  says the 2004 one is the primary and the 2009 reference may have been a confusion.
- grey: G2/G3 is given the same outcome as G3/N; the research says its pace and risk
  are not quantified. The Andalusian table's genotype shares are worked out from
  allele frequencies, assuming random mating. So is ryr1's Quarter Horse table.
- silver: chromosome 6 comes from a commercial laboratory's page. That a chestnut
  carrier has the same eye risk is reasoned from the genotype, not from a study that
  compared them. That silver is hidden on a chestnut is in words only.
- leopard still depends on patn2, an abstraction; no real second modifier was found.
- pdk4: G is flagged wild_type because the format needs one; no source says which
  allele is ancestral.
- slc5a3, ryr1, grey: the "general population" tables are still the mod's.
- Frequencies: brindle has none, on purpose. Published tables exist only for slc5a3
  (Fell, Dales), ryr1 (Quarter Horse), grey (Andalusian), b4galt7 (Friesian), dmrt3
  (Icelandic), gbe1 (Quarter Horse, Paint, Thoroughbred), myo5a (Arabian), plod1 (eight
  breeds), ppib (Quarter Horse), pax3 (Paint, Quarter Horse) and st14 (Akhal-Teke).
- agouti: At (seal brown) and A+ (wild bay) are in the data marked "invalid" (owner,
  2026-10-12). The research calls them plausible but unproven rather than wrong, and
  some commercial laboratories report At; "invalid" is the nearest tag the format has.
- Bay shade is now stated nowhere as a table: agouti gives bay or black, and the
  abstracted shade locus describes its effect in its summary only. The research
  supports a real shade modifier upstream of ASIP near RALY (chromosome 22), cause
  unidentified, with E/E bays tending darker than E/e.
- b4galt7: no chromosome was given. Its research names its sources by number only, so
  no citation was added; the two it already had (UC Davis, Leegwater et al.) are from
  the first export. The Friesian table omits d/d horses, which the 12 percent carrier
  estimate does not count.
- ednrb and hmga2 had one correction each and are otherwise unresearched.
- champagne: the names gold, amber and classic champagne depend on extension and agouti; the file says so in words and has no interactions table. "Sable" (on seal brown) and "ivory" (with cream) are registry names, not alleles, and are not in the data.
- ckm: a candidate that failed to replicate (Gu et al. 2010; no association in Quarter Horses). The file states no effect. (An earlier answer's "g.22999655C>A" for it was a slip for the DMRT3 variant; settled.) C is flagged wild_type only because the format needs one. Whether a locus with no established effect belongs in the data as published is the owner's call.
- dmrt3: the Icelandic table's genotype shares are worked out from one allele frequency, assuming random mating. A 2026 study reports further variants in exon 2 of DMRT3, of unknown effect; none is in the data. Other gait loci (trot versus pace in Standardbreds) are known to exist and are not modelled.
- dun: the wild-type flag moved from d2 to D (the research calls dun the ancestral state), and the d2/d2 outcome is now "non-dun", not "wild". nd1's markings are variable ("may show"), but d1/d1 and d1/d2 have one outcome that cannot say how often. The research gives no frequency figure, so the table is still the mod's. It gives no coordinate for the nd1 change. It does not mention the separate TBX3 height enhancer in the earlier note; that citation (PMC8796118) is left from the first export and nothing in the data rests on it.
- gbe1: the research says G/G foals are aborted late, stillborn or die as newborns. The format has one severity per condition, so it stays "lethal_at_birth" and the pair stays a living genotype, not a nonviable one. The breed tables give carriers as twice the allele frequency, among living horses; the research names the 2006 study only through the Merck manual and says later summaries put carriers at roughly 8-9%. No chromosome was given.
- kit: homozygotes. The 30 pairs the mod's export listed as nonviable, and W4/W4, are now living-table rows with the undocumented outcome "homozygote-not-observed": the research says none has been observed and that lethality is suspected (most for W1 to W4) but unproven. There is no nonviable list at this locus any more.
- kit: 601 pairs of two different variants are "compound-undocumented". Only booster pairs are given an outcome. That an all-white variant stays all white beside a booster is reasoned, not stated.
- kit: W37, W38 and W39 are published (2025; the owner's second answer) and now have outcomes, each resting on one family or one horse; the study calls them likely causes. W37 has only been seen on the same chromosome as W35, so W37/W35 is given the W37 outcome and W37/N is in the data as if it could occur. W36 is not assigned, like W29, and there is nothing above W39. The two answers both count 36 variants, for W1-W35 and for W1-W39; the counts were not reconciled.
- kit: W17 was split into W17a and W17b (owner, 2026-10-12), which the research lists as two missense variants. They are new alleles the mod lacks (mod-map.json, "not_in_mod"). The old W17 id is kept and marked "invalid"; its 41 pairs have the outcome "invalid-allele". The research does not say whether W17a and W17b are alternatives or sit together on one chromosome; they are treated as alternatives, and W17a/W17b is undocumented.
- kit: the research says a horse can carry several variants on one chromosome (W20 with W22, W19 with W34 and W35, W32 with W35, SB1 with W32), and that W22 has only been found with W20. The format has two alleles per locus and cannot say this; W22/N is in the data as if it could occur. The research also suggests SB1 as a locus of its own.
- kit: the mod named W4 "Camarillo white". The research ties Camarillo White lines to W1-W4 without saying which, so the name was dropped. The frequency table is still the mod's; no figure was given. tobiano was given what this research says about it; it has since had research of its own.
- matp: sunshine (sun) is kept as a disputed candidate, quality "unverified", not "invalid" (owner, 2026-10-12). Its five pairs have the undocumented outcome "sunshine-disputed". The research says the variant is at allele frequencies above 0.86 in Arabians, Belgians, Morgans, Thoroughbreds and Quarter Horses; no table was made, because the figure is a lower bound and the other alleles' shares were not given. The mod's "general population" table still has sunshine as very rare, which the research contradicts.
- matp: snowdrop (sno) is a published candidate known from very few horses; UC Davis does not test for it. sno/sno and the silent carrier are given outcomes on that report alone. Cr/sno and prl/sno are "compound-undocumented".
- matp: the names palomino, buckskin, cremello and the rest depend on extension and agouti; the file says so in words and has no interactions table. The first of the three research answers marks its sources by number only, with no addresses; the citations added are the addresses in the other two. The wild type is written N here; the research also gives n and C.
- gys1: one copy can show nothing at all (incomplete penetrance), but the format has one outcome per pair, so N/PSSM1 carries the condition and its description gives the range. No frequency table was made: the figures given (87% of affected draft-related horses, 72% of affected Quarter Horse-related horses, 18% of affected Warmbloods) are shares of diagnosed cases that carry the variant, not population frequencies. No chromosome was given. The allele id is P; UC Davis writes PSSM1.
- mushroom: the outcome depends on extension and agouti (clear on chestnut, uncertain on bay, none expected on black); the file says so in words and has no interactions table. Its link to flaxen is unresolved. No frequency was given, so the table is still the mod's "general population", though the research places the allele in Shetland Ponies and, rarely, Miniature Horses. The allele ids are Mu and mu; UC Davis writes Mu and N.
- met: met/met stays a nonviable pair. The research supports it as a recessive embryonic lethal, on indirect evidence: about 25 per cent lower insemination success in carrier-to-carrier matings. It is reported in Friesians only; the frequency table is still the mod's "general population". The research gave the UniProt record at two addresses; one is cited.
- myo5a: the locus now says it affects the coat, and the affected foal's outcome changes the coat, because the research describes the foal as diluted; the mod's export had both false. The Arabian tables use the mapping study's carrier shares (10.3% and 1.8%). The research also quotes UC Davis giving 10.3% and 1.6% as the allele's frequency; the two readings differ by a factor of two and were not reconciled. No chromosome was given.
- plod1: affected foals may be stillborn, and the research says some pregnancies may be lost early (unproven); the single severity stays "lethal_at_birth". The breed tables are carrier shares among horses tested; the small ones (Sport Pony, Haflinger, Knabstrupper) rest on 12 to 48 horses and assume "allele detected in n" means n carriers. No chromosome was given.
- ppib: the three Quarter Horse tables give carriers only; living affected horses exist and their share was not given, so they are counted with the clear horses. The research gives one address for all the frequency figures and does not say which study each comes from. The allele id is herda; laboratories write HR or H. No chromosome was given.
- extension: ea was added (the research: UC Davis recognises E, e and ea). It is a new allele the mod lacks (mod-map.json, "not_in_mod"). The interaction tables of agouti and flaxen gained its three pairs: ea/E as E/e, and ea/e and ea/ea as e/e. For flaxen that is reasoned from ea being a second red allele; no source describes flaxen on an ea horse. The frequency table is the mod's and has no ea; no figure was given. No chromosome and no variant coordinates were given.
- extension: the research says E/E bays may tend to be darker than E/e bays and that this is not established; it is in the summary only. It treats blood bay and liver bay as shades and seal brown (At) as unresolved, which agrees with agouti and the abstracted shade locus as they stand.
- shox: sa was split into Del1 and Del2 (owner, 2026-10-12), new alleles the mod lacks. The old sa id is kept and marked "invalid"; its four pairs have the outcome "invalid-allele", so the mod's own allele has no outcome here. The frequency table is the mod's and is still written for sa; no figure was given.
- shox has no "chromosome": the format takes a number, X or Y, and X or Y would make the locus sex-linked. It lies in the pseudoautosomal region of both and is inherited as autosomal; the summary says so.
- shox: the severity of skeletal atavism is "impairing", not "lethal_at_birth" (owner, 2026-10-12): the research describes foals born alive with severe deformity and never says they die at birth. Its phrase "nonviable or ethically untenable quality of life" is in the description.
- pax3: there is no nonviable list any more. SW4/SW4 is "splash-homozygote-undetected" (may be lethal, not demonstrated) and SW2/SW4 is "splash-undocumented" (never detected). No genotype carries a deafness condition: the research says SW2/SW2 horses "may be deaf" and that deafness is variable, which is the same rule mitf follows (only pairs a source confirms). The risk is in the descriptions.
- pax3: SW10 is in the data from the mod. The research says only that third-party sources mention further PAX3 variants outside UC Davis's SW1-SW8. It is named "unconfirmed", keeps quality "unverified" and has no outcome, as W37-W39 were treated before they were confirmed; whether it should be "invalid" like mitf SW9 is the owner's call. The two breed tables are worked out from one allele frequency each. The "general population" table is the mod's. The journal names in two citation labels were read off the addresses, not given by the research.
- tobiano: its own research and the kit research are both applied. The breeds in the summary are the two lists joined. The size of the inversion (about 43 Mb) and the ink spots come from the kit research; the tobiano answer gives neither. The frequency table is the mod's; no figure was given. The allele ids are To and to; UC Davis writes TO and N.
- tiger_eye: the research says tiger eye changes the iris and never the coat. The locus still says "affects_coat": true and its eye outcomes "changes_coat": true, because the eight eye-colour loci use those flags for anything drawn on the horse; what the flags should mean for eyes is a format question for the owner. TE2/TE2 rests on one horse. The frequency table is the mod's "general population", though the variants are known only from Puerto Rican Paso Finos; no figure was given.
- st14: the severity changed from "lethal_at_birth" to "lethal_in_early_life": the research says most affected foals died between a few weeks and three years old. That is longer than the format's "first weeks or months", and one horse was alive at nearly three; it is the nearest value, chosen by the session, not the owner. The locus is still "affects_coat": false, since it removes hair and does not change colour or pattern. The Akhal-Teke table rests on 191 horses and may not be a random sample. No chromosome was given. Most of the answer's sources are marked by number only.
- prkdc: the severity changed from "lethal_at_birth" to "lethal_in_early_life": the research says affected foals generally do not survive beyond about four to six months. No frequency table was made: the research gives roughly 0.2% of Arabians affected, says carrier figures differ widely, and advises against using the old percentages; the mod's "general population" table stands. The research marks its sources by number and lists them by name only, so no citation was added; the three it already had are from the first export. The variant has no coordinates or c. name.
- rapgef5: the owner's first answer found no horse allele of RAPGEF5 at all; her second, asked about EFIH by name, confirms the variant and the disorder, and the locus follows the second. The severity changed from "lethal_at_birth" to "lethal_in_early_life": signs are reported up to about 35 days old. Two Thoroughbred tables are given because the two studies disagree (1.8% and about 0.8% allele frequency); the answer also quotes carrier figures of 1.4-1.6% without a source of their own. The answer calls the change a "C-to-A transition"; C to A is a transversion, which changes nothing here.

INTERACTIONS
- Only agouti, leopard and flaxen carry an interactions table. agouti's is with
  extension alone since 2026-10-12.
- Real epistasis NOT stated in the data: grey and dominant white hiding other colours,
  cream on red versus black, silver showing only on black pigment.

STRUCTURE
- "chromosome" is only on loci whose research gave one. To list them:
  grep -l '"chromosome"' genes/*.json
- "sex" is not a locus here. brindle is x_linked and uses a placeholder allele "Y".
- The left-eye loci in the mod usually copy the right eye; that is not expressed here.
- tools/validate.mjs does not check that allele order is unchanged since the last
  version (there is no tagged version to compare with yet).
- 36 allele names keep the allele id, because the name alone would not tell two alleles
  of the locus apart ("Dominant white W22"). Others carry a laboratory's label in
  brackets ("Wild-type (G1)").
