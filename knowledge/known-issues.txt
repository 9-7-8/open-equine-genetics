KNOWN ISSUES IN THE DATA
========================
As of the first export, 2026-10-12. Nothing in genes/ has been audited. Remove a line here
when it is fixed, and say so in the session log.

HOW THE EXPORT WAS MADE
- A throwaway Java program read the mod's compiled gene registry and wrote each locus
  (knowledge/how-this-was-exported/). The pair tables, nonviable pairs, conditions and
  frequencies are therefore exactly what the mod computes, at mod commit 93af89d9 plus one
  docs commit. Nothing was typed by hand except the items listed under BY HAND.
- tools/validate.mjs passes on all 68 files (structure only: it cannot tell whether the
  science is right). It was broken on purpose twice and failed both times.

BY HAND, UNCHECKED
- "symbol" on the 43 published loci was filled from the exporting session's own knowledge,
  not from a source. Check each against UC Davis VGL or OMIA. Particular doubts: "roan" and
  "tobiano" are given KIT, but both are KIT-linked rather than known KIT coding variants;
  "patn1" is given RFWD3; "tiger_eye" SLC24A5; "mushroom" MFSD12; "brindle" MBTPS2.
- The name and summary of the five abstracted body loci.

WRITTEN FOR A GAME
- Summaries and phenotype descriptions come from the mod. Many are good plain science;
  some describe game effects. Files known to mention the game ("the mod", hearts of
  health, golden carrots): abstracted_hardiness, acan, b4galt7, cvm, fertility, gbe1,
  hmga2, lama3, lcorl, leopard, mstn, ryr1, ryr2, tiger_eye, toe1.
- The five abstracted body loci: phenotype descriptions talk about "the percentage written
  on that copy". In the mod each allele copy carries its own inherited strength. That idea
  is not in this format. Decide whether it belongs here.
- knowledge/science/*.txt are copies of wiki pages and say so at the top. They explain
  the real science AND how the mod departs from it.

COLLAPSED AND MISSING REAL SCIENCE
- The mod merges several real loci into one and drops some real alleles on purpose. Known:
  kit (dominant white W alleles, sabino, and more, as one locus), mitf, matp. These are
  exported in the mod's shape. Uncollapsing is audit work.
- Real alleles the science notes name that the data lacks, e.g. the "ea" allele at
  extension. Find them by reading each science note against its gene file.
- Dosage loci (shade, flaxen, sooty, pangare, markings, grey) are the mod's model.

FREQUENCIES
- Every table is "estimated". They are the mod's wild-horse founder rates. A few were set
  from published surveys (the kit table cites a tested population in the mod's source
  comments) but none carries its source here.
- Breed-specific frequencies do not exist here until breeds are exported.
- brindle has one table per sex; patn1 and patn2 have one with and one without the
  leopard allele. The left-eye loci in the mod usually copy the right eye; that is NOT
  expressed here - each eye locus has only its free-standing table.

CITATIONS
- 29 of 68 loci have citations. They are every external link found in that gene's wiki
  Science tab, attached to the locus as a whole, not to an allele or a claim.
- 39 loci have none. Their science notes may still name papers in prose.
- "source" was guessed from the link's host name.
- patn1 and patn2 share the leopard page in the mod's wiki; see knowledge/science/leopard.txt.
- 15 loci have no science note at all: the five abstracted body loci, the eight eye loci,
  patn1, patn2.

INTERACTIONS
- Only agouti, leopard and flaxen carry an interactions table. agouti's includes the
  mod's split of bay into shades by the abstracted "shade" locus, which is the mod's model.
- Other real epistasis is NOT stated in the data: grey and dominant white hiding other
  colours, cream on red versus black, silver showing only on black pigment. The mod does
  these in its painting code, so the export could not see them.

STRUCTURE
- "sex" is not a locus here. brindle is x_linked and uses a placeholder allele "Y".
- acan lists a lethal homozygote as a genotype with a condition, while kit, met, mitf and
  pax3 list theirs under nonviable_genotypes. That mirrors the mod (lethal at birth versus
  never conceived). Check that the difference is real for each.
- tools/validate.mjs does not check that allele order is unchanged since the last version
  (it has no earlier version to compare with until this is a git repository).
