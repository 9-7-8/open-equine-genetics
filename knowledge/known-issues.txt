STILL WRONG OR UNPROVEN IN THE RESEARCHED LOCI
- abstracted_jump_strength was "ryr2" until 2026-10-12 (owner: rename it). The data has two abstractions for jumping, this and abstracted_jump; whether both are wanted is open.
- acan: D5 is "unconfirmed" with no outcome: proposed in a dissertation and tested by one laboratory (CombiBreed), with no peer-reviewed report. UC Davis has no ACAN test page. Two of Generatio's four positions do not match the file's D3 and D4 (question G7). D1/D1, D1/D2 and D1/D3 are nonviable; D1/D4 is a living pair with its own outcome, usually lost before birth but known once from a live foal (the format cannot give a pair a chance of surviving). Pairs of D2, D3 and D4 are born alive; D2 pairs milder, but the format has one severity for the dwarf condition. The Miniature Horse table is worked out from per-allele frequencies (sum 0.17), and counts D1/D4 at its random-mating share; a Brazilian study gives 0.280 for the four together. Who reported the 0.280 and the 1.43-inch height study is in doubt (question G6). A Brazilian candidate variant, c.6465A>T (p.Leu2155Phe), "potentially associated", is not an allele here.
- agouti: At (seal brown) and A+ (wild bay) are "unverified", proposed alleles with no outcome (owner, 2026-10-15); the looks are real, the alleles unvalidated. The research suggests the looks be modelled as separate modifiers of unknown basis; only the abstracted shade locus exists, and nothing for wild bay's low leg points.
- b4galt7: its first research names its sources by number only. The Friesian tables omit d/d horses. The 12% is Leegwater et al. 2016's 22 of 177, a validation set, not a random sample; KFPS testing gave about 15% in 2000 and 12.5% in 2021 (Hermans et al. 2024). A Brazilian 33% (12 horses) is not a table. Round four confirms all of this and adds a 2026 screen of 8,263 Friesians (BMC Genomics) that gave no B4GALT7 figure alone and no address. The Friesian Health Panel has no address either.
- champagne: the names gold, amber and classic champagne depend on extension and agouti; the file says so in words and has no interactions table. "Sable" and "ivory" are registry names, not alleles.
- ckm: a candidate that failed to replicate (Gu et al. 2010; no association in Quarter Horses); the file states no effect. Its reference allele is flagged wild_type and called ancestral on that alone. Chromosome 10 (round four; round three's 9 was an error). The Italian Trotter table rests on taking g.16079732G>A (EquCab3.0) as the file's g.15884567G>A (EquCab2.0): best supported, but no source states the conversion. Thoroughbred G 0.50 is from 20 horses, not tabled. Whether a locus with no established effect belongs in the data as published is the owner's call.
- dmrt3: the Icelandic table is worked out from one allele frequency. Of six exon-2 variants in a 2026 study, only c.902C>A has an established effect; three are not named in the summary the research could reach. Trot versus pace in Standardbreds is predicted by a seven-SNP panel of putative modifiers (McCoy et al. 2019); not modelled.
- dun: nd1's markings are variable ("may show"), but d1/d1 and d1/d2 share one outcome that cannot say how often; round two says d1/d1 may mark more consistently, unmeasured. The nd1 change is G>T at chr8:18,227,267+1,066 on EquCab2.0, inside the 1.6 kb sequence the reference lacks, so it has no plain reference position. Breed tables exist for the Norwegian Fjord and Gypsy Vanner only; the rest are in Avila et al.'s Supplementary Table S4 (question B3). A citation to a TBX3 height enhancer (PMC8796118) has nothing in the data resting on it.
- ednrb: the EDNRB gene span an answer gave is on another assembly (NC_091700.1) and is not in the data.
- extension: the "general population" table has no ea. Breed tables exist for the Pony of the Americas only; ea is at 0.035 in Knabstruppers, with no e figure beside it, and e at 0.12 in Percherons, with only an unfiltered ea count beside it (question B3).
- gbe1: G/G foals are aborted late, stillborn or die as newborns; the format has one severity per condition, so it stays "lethal_at_birth" and the pair stays living, not nonviable. The Quarter Horse and Paint tables give UC Davis's carrier estimates (8.3%, 7.1%); UC Davis now gives 8-11% by discipline.
- grey: G2/G3 has the G3/N outcome, as UC Davis states. The Andalusian table, and ryr1's Quarter Horse table, are worked out from allele frequencies.
- gys1: one copy can show nothing (incomplete penetrance), but the format has one outcome per pair, so N/P carries the condition and its text gives the range. Drafts are given only as a 36-92% range, not tabled. The allele id is P; UC Davis writes PSSM1.
- hmga2: the estimated "general population" table gives p a 25% allele share, though the allele is found in no full-sized breed tested.
- kit: homozygotes of 30 variants, and W4/W4, are living rows with the undocumented outcome "homozygote-not-observed": none has been observed, and lethality is suspected (most for W1-W4) but unproven.
- kit: 601 pairs of two different variants are "compound-undocumented". Only booster pairs have an outcome; that an all-white variant stays all white beside a booster is reasoned, not stated.
- kit: horses can carry several variants on one chromosome (W20 with W22, W35 with W37, W19 with W34 and W35, W32 with W35, SB1 with W32); the format has two alleles per locus and cannot say this. W22 stands for the W20_22 haplotype (it is never found without W20). W37 has only been seen with W35, yet W37/N is in the data as if it could occur. W20/W22 is "boosted-white", "usually all white" in words. The research suggests SB1 as a locus of its own.
- kit: W17 is the allele again, two changes always together (owner, 2026-10-15); W17a and W17b are invalid. The OMIA position quoted for it does not match the c. changes base for base and is not in the data.
- kit: W37, W38 and W39 (2025) each rest on one family or one horse. W29 and W36 are not assigned; nothing above W39. W20 is at chr3:79,548,220 T>C on EquCab3.0 (OMIA, rs1143160924; round four), in the summary; the 2024 paper prints it a digit short, and round three's 79,482,200 was wrong. No other KIT position is in the data. Sixteen breed tables from Avila et al. 2022 (round four), for breeds with W20 alone among the tested variants; W13, W32, W34, W35 and later variants were never tested and count as N. No table where SB1 is found (its per-breed figure is in the supplement) or W22 (Avila et al. did not phase W20_22, and its W20 count includes W20 on W22 chromosomes, so the data's W20 and W22 cannot be split from it).
- lama3, lamc2: no frequency question left; JEB1 and JEB2 tables are in.
- leopard depends on patn2, an abstraction; more than PATN1 modifies the pattern, and nothing else is named.
- matp: sunshine is a disputed candidate with the undocumented outcome "sunshine-disputed". The variant is the major allele in every breed group sequenced (above 0.86 in five breeds, Durward-Akhurst et al. 2024), but whether it dilutes is unresolved; with no exact figure per breed it is in no table (owner, 2026-10-15), so the tables count its copies as N. Snowdrop is known from one Gypsy Cob family; no Cr/sno or prl/sno horse is reported anywhere, so those pairs stay undocumented. The general table still gives sno small shares. The names palomino, buckskin and the rest depend on extension and agouti, in words only. Lusitano is the only breed table (Cr and prl worked out from Avila et al.); other breeds' Cr figures are in Avila et al.'s Supplementary Table S4 (question B3). The first matp answer cites by number only.
- met: met/met is nonviable on indirect evidence (lower insemination success in carrier matings). Reported in Friesians only; no frequency (question G4). Friesian hydrocephalus (B3GALNT2) is not a locus here.
- mitf: SW9 (McFadden et al. 2023) rests on association in one breed. 24 pairs of two different variants are "splash-undocumented" and 6 homozygotes "splash-homozygote-undetected". No genotype carries a deafness condition (owner: only pairs a source confirms). Thirteen breed tables from Avila et al. 2022, which tested SW1, SW3, SW5 and SW6 only; SW7, SW8 and SW9 count as N.
- mushroom: the outcome depends on extension and agouti (clear on chestnut, uncertain on bay, none on black), in words only. Its link to flaxen is unresolved.
- myo5a: UC Davis's "1.6%" for non-Egyptian Arabians matches neither of the mapping study's figures and is left out. The Egyptian farm table (0.08) is one farm, partly targeted.
- pax3: SW10 rests on association in one breed (30 PRE horses studied, two with SW10 reported); no frequency is published. SW4/SW4 and SW10/SW10 are "splash-homozygote-undetected"; pairs of two variants "splash-undocumented". No deafness condition on any pair; the risk is in the text. The journal names in two citation labels were read off the addresses.
- pdk4: A is flagged wild_type (reference allele, carried by pre-domestication horses); no formal reconstruction was found.
- plod1: affected foals may be stillborn and some pregnancies may be lost early (unproven); the severity stays "lethal_at_birth". No EquCab3.0 position for c.2032G>A. Several Warmblood breeds have no figure (question G2).
- ppib: the Quarter Horse tables give carriers only; living affected horses are counted with the clear ones, and no observed affected share is published. One table comes from a research-summary site.
- prkdc: four Arabian tables (US 1998 8.4%, Iran 2015 1.6%, Turkish state studs 0 of 239, Egypt 2018 0 of 33); the newest result, a pooled 2026 sequencing of 120 Arabians, found none but gives no share. Durward-Akhurst et al. 2024's Arabian figure is in a supplement not retrieved. The first research names its sources by number only.
- rapgef5: three Thoroughbred tables from three studies, all kept.
- roan: the symbol stays KIT; the causal variant is unknown. RN1-RN3 are haplotype markers, in the summary only.
- ryr1, slc5a3, grey: their "general population" tables are estimates.
- shox: no "chromosome": it lies in the pseudoautosomal region and is inherited as autosomal; the format's X or Y would make it sex-linked. The severity "lethal_at_birth" stands for "usually nonviable or put down"; the format has one severity. The "general population" table is still written for the invalid sa.
- silver: the one-copy eye condition sits on every Z/z pair, because the format cannot tell a chestnut carrier from a bay one; the risk is not established for a chestnut carrier, and the text says so.
- st14: "lethal_in_early_life" was the session's choice, the nearest value for death between a few weeks and three years. The Akhal-Teke table (26 of 191) is the only survey. Most of the first answer's sources are numbered only.
- tiger_eye: the research says tiger eye changes the iris, never the coat, but the locus says "affects_coat": true, as all eight eye loci do for anything drawn on the horse; what the flag means for eyes is a format question for the owner. TE2/TE2 rests on one horse. No population frequency (question B3).
- tobiano: TO/TO averages very slightly more white than TO/N (36% against 34%, overlapping), but the format has one outcome for both; the share of white is in words only. How the inversion alters KIT, and the modifiers of the amount of white, are unknown. The 36.7 Mb from the EquCab3.0 positions and the review's 43 Mb are both in the text.
- toe1: the variant may act through MUTYH; the mechanism is not settled. The Egyptian sample (6.25% carriers) is far below the Arabian table's 20%.
- Breed names given in round-two summaries are the answer's descriptions, to be treated as estimates.
- Round two gave three addresses in no gene file: a game's wiki (horsereality) for pearl, a breeder's tool page for fragile foal, and UC Davis's PSSM1 page for HERDA.

  I maintain an open database of horse genetics. For each question below:
  - give the address (URL or DOI) of every source you use;
  - for any frequency, say whether it is an allele frequency or a share of carriers, and
