Assembly and chromosome notes
- B4GALT7 c.50G>A is also reported on the older EquCab2.0 assembly as
  ECA14:g.4535550C>T; the EquCab3.0 coordinate is ECA14:g.3772591C>T.
  (https://omia.org/OMIA002068/9796/)
- MYO5A and PPIB are both on ECA1 in EquCab3.0, but are far apart: about 139.29 Mb
  versus 129.31 Mb, respectively. (https://omia.org/variant/omia.variant:510/)
- SCN4A is on ECA11 in the horse; the human SCN4A gene is on human chromosome 17, so
  human and horse chromosome numbers should not be interchanged.
  (https://omia.org/variant/omia.variant:162/)
- For PLOD1, the chromosome and gene span are well established, but I was unable to
  retrieve a verified single-base EquCab3.0 g. coordinate for c.2032G>A from the
  available sources; I would not guess one. UC Davis confirms the variant nomenclature
  and its association with Fragile Foal Syndrome. (https://omia.org/OMIA001982/9796/)

Quick variant identities
- B4GALT7 c.50G>A - Friesian dwarfism. (https://omia.org/OMIA002068/9796/)
- MYO5A c.4249del - lavender foal syndrome. (https://omia.org/variant/omia.variant:510/)
- PLOD1 c.2032G>A - Fragile Foal Syndrome type 1 / former WFFS.
  (https://ceh.vetmed.ucdavis.edu/health-topics/warmblood-fragile-foal-syndrome-type-1-wffs)
- PPIB c.115G>A - HERDA. (https://omia.org/variant/omia.variant:157/)
- SCN4A c.4248C>G - HYPP. (https://vgl.ucdavis.edu/test/hypp)
- ST14 c.388G>T - Naked foal syndrome. (https://omia.org/variant/omia.variant:354/)

What was done with it (the session, 2026-10-15)
- Each of the six loci gained its chromosome. Five gained their EquCab3.0 position in
  the summary; PLOD1 has none.
- MYO5A: the summary had g.138235715del, from an earlier answer that named no assembly.
  It now gives g.139290592del on EquCab3.0, from OMIA. The old figure is in
  known-issues; it may be an EquCab2 position, which was not checked.
- PLOD1: the gene span is given on NC_091685.1. EquCab3.0's chromosome sequences are
  the NC_0091xx.3 series (as the other rows show), so that span may be on a different
  assembly. It is not in the data.
