Zebra striping - science notes
(A science note on this locus, written 2026-10-12. Not yet audited.)

Which way round a zebra is
--------------------------

Ask which of a zebra's two colours is the animal and which is the marking, and the biology answers plainly. Hair follicles carry melanocytes; in the dark bands they deposit melanin into the growing hair, and in the white ones signalling suppresses that. The white stripe is not a white pigment - it is an absence of the dark one, and the dark state is the default. A zebra is a dark animal with the colour taken out in bands.

Not modelled
------------

- Species. There is one locus, not a plains / mountain / Grévy's trichotomy.
- Facial stripes that curve round the eye and nostril.
- A reaction-diffusion simulation. Zebra striping is usually modelled as a Turing pattern - local chemical signals producing repeated spacing without any gene specifying "stripe number 17 here". One consequence of the Turing account is that the pattern changes shape with body geometry.
