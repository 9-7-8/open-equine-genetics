LAMA3 - science notes
(Copied 2026-10-12 from the Science tab of the Procedural Horse Coats wiki page for this gene.
Written for that project: it may mention how a game models the gene. Not yet audited.)

At a glance
-----------

Gene | LAMA3 - laminin subunit alpha 3, another chain of the same laminin-332 trimer
Variant | A partial deletion, distinct from the LAMC2 variant of the draft breeds
Disorder | Junctional epidermolysis bullosa (JEB), American Saddlebred form
Inheritance | Autosomal recessive
Measured frequency | 9 carriers in 175 randomly sampled 2007 foals; allele frequency 0.026
Heterozygote | Clinically normal carrier

The same structure, a different chain
-------------------------------------

Laminin-332 is a trimer of three chains - alpha 3, beta 3 and gamma 2 - and it is the central anchoring protein holding the epidermis to the basement membrane beneath it. LAMC2 supplies the gamma chain; this locus supplies the alpha.

Break either and the same assembly fails, which is why the two produce a clinically indistinguishable disease: blistering and skin loss at every point of mechanical stress, and detachment of the hoof capsules, from birth. The mechanism is set out in more detail on the LAMC2 page and is not repeated here.

The variants themselves are not alike. The Saddlebred form is a partial deletion of LAMA3; the draft form is a separate mutation in a separate gene. Two horses can each be "a JEB carrier" and have nothing genetically in common beyond the sentence.

Why the breed societies test
----------------------------

Because the carrier is invisible and the homozygote is unsalvageable, the only useful intervention happens before the mating. An allele frequency of 0.026 sounds small, but it means roughly one Saddlebred in twenty carries a copy - so two carriers meeting is not a freak event in a population that breeds within itself, and one in four of their foals would be affected.

That is the same argument the mod makes with its own machinery: the carrier row costs nothing at all, and the entire cost of the locus sits behind a pairing. It is what makes a pedigree and a gene database entry worth keeping rather than flavour text.
