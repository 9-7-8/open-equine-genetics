ROUND THREE (the owner's answer to question G6, last part, 2026-10-15)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Asked whether UC Davis has an ACAN test page and which variants laboratories name. No
address has been opened by a maintainer. Her answer, condensed, with every point and
address she gave:

  - No UC Davis VGL page for ACAN dwarfism was found: its horse-test catalogue and site
    search show none. (https://vgl.ucdavis.edu/dna-tests/horse) Do not cite UC Davis as
    the testing source for ACAN.
  - Generatio (a European laboratory) tests for four variants, which it calls
    pathogenic, and says they give a very similar phenotype - abnormal cartilage growth,
    skeletal problems, painful dwarfism - mainly in Miniature Horses and Shetland Ponies.
    (https://generatio.de/en/guidance/lexicon/acan-dwarfism-chondrodysplasia-acan-horse)
      D1   g.95291270del
      D2   g.95284530C>T
      D3*  g.95282140C>G
      D4   g.95257458_95257500del
  - N/Dx: clinically normal carrier. Dx/Dx: affected. The answer suggests treating any
    two variant alleles as affected, as its own modelling advice, not a finding.
  - ACAN encodes aggrecan, a cartilage protein: effects are skeletal (height, bones,
    joints, perhaps hoof shape through limb angles), with no direct effect on muscle,
    speed, feathering or pigment.

The session's reading (not the answer's):
  - The assembly of the four g. positions is not named.
  - Two of them do not match the variants in the gene file. D3 here is a substitution
    (C>G); the file's D3, from earlier research, is a one-base deletion (c.6700delC).
    D4 here deletes 43 bases (95257458-95257500); the file's D4 (c.7299_7319del)
    deletes 21. The asterisk on Generatio's "D3*" is not explained. Asked again (G7).
  - D5's reported position (g.95258999C>T) lies among these, between D4 and D3.
    Generatio's list of four does not include it.
  - How long D1/D1 foals live, whether other pairs reach adulthood, and carrier
    frequencies were not answered (G6 stays open for those).

What the gene file does with it: the summary says no UC Davis test page was found and
names Generatio's test; one citation. Allele names unchanged.

- No well-established published "ACAN D5" allele was found. The answer says to treat D5 as

  file keeps D5 as unconfirmed, with no outcome.
- The established work concerns a small set of recessive variants in Miniature Horses and
  related ponies.

  horse test index.
- D1/D1 is associated with severe skeletal dwarfism and is generally incompatible with
  normal life, but "lethal at birth" should be stated cautiously because survival time can
  vary. Whether the other dwarf pairs survive was not answered.

Why this is not "is it homozygous?"
-----------------------------------

A D1/D4 horse is affected. So is D2/D3. The five variants are different broken versions of the same gene, and what matters is that the horse has no working copy, not that its two copies match.

Every other disorder in this mod can be written as "count the variant allele and check for two". This one cannot, and the check has to be a predicate over the whole pair. It is the clearest argument in the model for a combination table over a dominance label: recessive describes nine of these ten affected combinations and quietly loses the compound heterozygotes.

What that means at the paddock gate
Two carriers of different variants are exactly as dangerous as two carriers of the same one. "My two lines carry different mutations" is not the safety it sounds like.
