WHERE THE SCIENCE COMES FROM
============================
Written by the exporting session from general knowledge, 2026-10-12. Check each address
before relying on it; none was opened while writing this.

IN ORDER OF AUTHORITY FOR THIS REPO
1. UC Davis Veterinary Genetics Laboratory (vgl.ucdavis.edu). The owner's named reference.
   Its horse test pages give, per test: the gene, the alleles it reports, the inheritance,
   the phenotype, the breeds it is found in, and sometimes allele frequencies. A test page
   is the best single source for "is this allele real and what is it called".
   Source value in a citation: "ucdavis-vgl".
2. OMIA, Online Mendelian Inheritance in Animals (omia.org). One entry per trait per
   species, with the causal variant table and the reference list. Best for disorders and
   for finding the primary paper. Source value: "omia".
3. The primary paper, through PubMed or PubMed Central. Needed for frequencies in a
   population, for the exact variant, and whenever 1 and 2 disagree. Source value:
   "pubmed" for an NCBI address, "paper" for a journal address.
4. Breed registries and breed-association rules, for which colours a breed admits and for
   height standards. Source value: "other". Say which registry in the label.
5. Everything else (textbooks, review sites, Wikipedia): a lead to follow, not a citation.

HOW FAR TO TRUST WHAT
- A commercial test existing for an allele is strong evidence the allele is real. It says
  nothing about how common it is.
- A frequency from one study describes that study's sample: breed, country, year, and how
  the horses were chosen. Tested populations are biased toward horses whose owners
  suspected something. Record the population in the table's "population" field.
- A dominant white (W) allele is often known from one family. Its frequency in the general
  population is effectively zero; do not invent one.
- Nomenclature changes. Record the name UC Davis uses as the allele name, and keep older
  names in the summary so a search still finds them.

WHAT THE MOD'S OWN NOTES CONTAIN
